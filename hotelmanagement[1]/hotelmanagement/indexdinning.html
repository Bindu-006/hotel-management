RESTAURANT BRANDS

Step into the realm of our culinary legends where a symphony of flavours enchants your taste buds, ambience embraces you in a tapestry of elegance and the genuine warmth of our service leaves you feeling truly indulged.

MACHAN

Presenting a delightful blend of earthy simplicity and urban sophistication, Machan invites its guests to enjoy a diverse bill of fare that celebrates the best of Indian and international cuisine.

LOYA

LOYA takes its diners on a gastronomic journey through North India's diverse landscape, blending flavours from the Himalayan foothills to the deserts of Rajasthan. The restaurant's name, which means 'to unite' in Hindi, reflects its mission to bring together the best of Indian cuisine.
