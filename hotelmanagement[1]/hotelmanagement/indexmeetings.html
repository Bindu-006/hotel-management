MEETINGS & CONFERENCES

Personalised, unforgettable events.

Conveniently located on MG Road, within three acres of landscaped gardens, The Rajputana Palace, Rajasthan is the perfect location to celebrate a special occasion or host a corporate event.

From birthdays to baby showers, engagements and anniversaries, we have a venue to suit and the service to match. Our dedicated team will take care of the special details that will bring your event to life.

Having hosted car and product launches to business meetings, conferences and seminars; we are experienced in managing corporate events of world class standards.

At our business centre, we offer three meeting rooms for up to six people, and a board room for twelve.

Hibiscus & Hyacinth.

Interconnected and separated by a soundproof, sliding wooden door, Hibiscus & Hyacinth Halls are some of the finest banquet halls in Bengaluru; a charming venue for functions with your closest family and friends, or esteemed colleagues.
