Welcome to Rajasthan.

Located in the northwest of India, Rajasthan showcases a captivating blend of majestic Rajputana architecture, from formidable forts and ornate palaces to vibrant havelis, all set against the unique and expansive landscape of the Thar Desert.

Inspired by this rich regal heritage, The Rajputana Palace in Rajasthan offers a sanctuary where traditional grandeur meets modern comfort. Our hotel provides an authentic experience of the 'Land of Kings' with contemporary hospitality.

Committed to preserving Rajasthan's natural and cultural heritage, The Rajputana Palace embraces sustainable practices including water conservation and support for local artisans. We aim to offer an enriching and responsible travel experience in this land of timeless charm.

Where would you like to dine?

Seated under a vaulted dome in one of our exceptional restaurants. On a perfect terrace overlooking the garden and the lake beyond. A bespoke candle-lit dinner for two with the stars above.

Suryamahal and Chandni.

Suryamahal is our daytime dining area, serving Western and Indian cuisine under a sky blue, cloud brushed dome. Chandni is the adjoining al fresco dining terrace with a sweeping view of the City Palace and Lake Pichola. At night, Chandni is a candle-lit mosaic of canopied tables filled with the sweet sound of live, traditional music.

Cuisine: Indian, Western
Hours: 7:00 AM - 11:00 PM
Location: Main Building, Ground Floor

Udaimahal Restaurant.

Udaimahal offers an authentic fine dining experience with traditional Rajasthani and North Indian cuisine. The restaurant's ornate décor, featuring intricate mirror work and hand-painted murals, creates a regal ambiance reminiscent of Udaipur's royal heritage.
